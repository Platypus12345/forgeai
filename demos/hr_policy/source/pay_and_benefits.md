MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - SHORE STAFF PAY AND BENEFITS
Document HR-09, revision 4, effective 1 April 2026

1. SALARY PAYMENT
Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th of the month, or the last working day
before the 25th where that date falls on a weekend or public holiday. Payslips
are published to the HR portal two working days before payment.

2. OVERTIME
Overtime applies to staff in grades 1 to 5 only. Hours worked beyond 40 in a
week are paid at 1.5 times the basic hourly rate. Hours worked on a public
holiday are paid at 2.0 times the basic hourly rate. Overtime must be approved
in advance by a line manager and claimed within 30 days of being worked.
Grades 6 and above receive no overtime payment; time off in lieu may be agreed.

3. PENSION
Meridian operates a defined contribution scheme. The employee contributes a
minimum of 5 per cent of pensionable pay and Meridian contributes 8 per cent.
Employees contributing 7 per cent or more receive an enhanced employer
contribution of 10 per cent. Auto-enrolment occurs after 3 months of service.
Employees may opt out within 30 days of enrolment.

4. PROBATION AND NOTICE
The probation period is 6 months for all shore staff and may be extended once
by up to 3 months. During probation either party may terminate with 1 week
notice. After probation, employees must give 4 weeks written notice of
resignation. Meridian gives 4 weeks notice, rising to 1 week per completed year
of service to a maximum of 12 weeks.

5. EXPENSES
Business expenses must be claimed within 30 days, with receipts, through the
HR portal. Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at 0.45 per mile for the
first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 0.25 per mile thereafter. Standard class
rail travel is the default; business class air travel requires Director
approval and applies only to flights over 6 hours.

6. TRAINING AND PROFESSIONAL FEES
Meridian reimburses one professional membership fee per employee per year up to
450. Approved training courses are funded in full where they are relevant to
the role. Staff who leave within 12 months of completing a course costing more
than 2,000 repay 50 per cent of the cost.

7. OTHER BENEFITS
Private medical cover is provided to grades 4 and above after 12 months of
service. Life assurance is provided to all staff at 4 times annual salary. The
cycle to work scheme is open to all staff after probation.
