MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - WORKPLACE AND CONDUCT POLICY
Document HR-11, revision 2, effective 1 April 2026

1. HYBRID WORKING
Shore staff are required to attend their contractual office location for a
minimum of 3 days per week. Core hours, during which staff must be contactable,
are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Requests to vary the hybrid pattern are made
through the HR portal and are reviewed every 6 months.

2. HOME WORKING EQUIPMENT
A one-off home working equipment allowance of 350 is available to staff who
have completed probation. The allowance covers a desk, chair, monitor and
peripherals. Equipment remains the property of Meridian and must be returned on
termination. Display screen equipment assessments are mandatory and repeat
every 2 years.

3. ACCEPTABLE USE OF IT
Company devices are for business use; incidental personal use is permitted.
Company data must not be stored on personal cloud accounts or removable media.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and multi-factor authentication is
mandatory for all remote access. Suspected security incidents must be reported
to the IT service desk within 1 hour of discovery.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
Staff must declare any conflict of interest within 5 working days of it
arising. Gifts and hospitality with a value above 50 must be declared in the
gifts register, and gifts above 250 may not be accepted. Facilitation payments
are prohibited without exception.

5. DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
The procedure has four stages: informal discussion, written warning, final
written warning, and dismissal. A written warning remains live for 6 months; a
final written warning remains live for 12 months. Employees have the right to
be accompanied at any formal meeting by a colleague or trade union
representative. Gross misconduct may result in dismissal without notice and
includes theft, violence, and deliberate breach of safety rules.

6. GRIEVANCES
Grievances should first be raised informally with the line manager. A formal
grievance must be submitted in writing and will be acknowledged within 5
working days, with a hearing held within 15 working days. An employee may
appeal the outcome within 10 working days of being notified.

7. ANTI-HARASSMENT
Meridian does not tolerate harassment or discrimination. Reports may be made to
any manager, to HR, or through the confidential reporting line. All reports are
investigated, and the complainant is informed of the outcome in writing.
