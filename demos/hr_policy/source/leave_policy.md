MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - SHORE STAFF LEAVE POLICY
Document HR-04, revision 7, effective 1 April 2026

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all shore-based employees of Meridian Maritime Services
on permanent or fixed-term contracts of six months or longer. It does not apply
to seagoing personnel, agency contractors, or interns.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
Full-time shore staff accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year, in
addition to 8 public holidays. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.
Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days per completed calendar month of service.
Part-time staff accrue pro rata on the basis of contracted hours.
Staff with 5 or more continuous years of service accrue 28 days.

3. BOOKING AND NOTICE
Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 14 calendar days
before the first day of absence. Absences of 10 consecutive working days or
more require 30 calendar days notice. A line manager must respond within 5
working days. Leave may be refused where fewer than 60 per cent of a team would
remain available.

4. CARRY-OVER
A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the next leave year. Carried
days expire on 30 June and are not paid in lieu. Carry-over above 5 days is
permitted only where leave was refused for operational reasons, and requires
written approval from the Head of Department.

5. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive up to 10 days of fully paid sick leave per leave year. A
self-certification form is required for absences of 1 to 3 days. A medical
certificate is required from the 4th consecutive day of absence. Absence must
be reported to the line manager by 09:30 on the first day.
After 10 days, statutory sick pay applies for a maximum of 28 weeks.

6. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are entitled to 18 weeks of leave at full pay, followed by
up to 26 weeks unpaid. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks at full
pay. Notice of at least 15 weeks before the expected date is required.
Entitlement begins after 26 weeks of continuous service.

7. UNPAID AND SPECIAL LEAVE
Compassionate leave of up to 5 days per event is granted for the death of an
immediate family member. Jury service is granted as paid leave for up to 10
days. Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be requested after 2 years of service
and is at the discretion of the Head of Department.

8. LEAVE ON TERMINATION
Accrued but untaken leave is paid at the final salary rate. Leave taken in
excess of accrual is deducted from the final payment.
