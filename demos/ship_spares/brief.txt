We need a procurement assistant for our technical purchasing team who buy ship
spares. It should answer questions about the spares catalogue - part numbers,
prices, lead times, stock on hand, which supplier - and about our procurement
rules: who approves what value, how many quotations we need, the emergency
procedure, and each supplier's payment and warranty terms. It needs to work out
order totals and discounts, and tell people whether a delivery date is realistic
given today's date and the lead time. It must not negotiate with suppliers or
commit the company to anything.
