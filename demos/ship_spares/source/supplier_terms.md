MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - SUPPLIER COMMERCIAL TERMS SUMMARY
Document PR-07, revision 3, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This summary records the agreed commercial terms with each approved supplier on
the ship spares framework. Where a supplier quotation conflicts with this
summary, this summary prevails unless varied in writing.

2. NORDHAVN MARINE SUPPLY
Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Incoterms are FCA Rotterdam.
Warranty is 12 months from delivery or 2,000 running hours, whichever occurs
first. Orders above USD 20,000 attract a 3 per cent volume discount. Returns of
unused stock are accepted within 30 days subject to a 15 per cent restocking
fee. On-time delivery performance for 2025 was 91 per cent.

3. KOBE TURBO SYSTEMS
Payment terms are 45 days from invoice date. Incoterms are CIP destination
port. Warranty is 18 months from delivery for turbocharger assemblies and 12
months for all other items. No volume discount applies. Returns are accepted
only for items shipped in error. Lead times above 45 days require a 30 per cent
deposit at order. On-time delivery performance for 2025 was 78 per cent, and
the supplier is currently under review.

4. BALTIC DIESEL PARTS
Payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Incoterms are FCA Gdansk.
Warranty is 12 months from delivery. A 5 per cent discount applies to orders
above USD 10,000 placed against the annual framework. Returns are accepted
within 14 days with no restocking fee. On-time delivery performance for 2025
was 94 per cent.

5. HARBOUR CHANDLERS LTD
Payment terms are 14 days from invoice date. Incoterms are DAP vessel.
Warranty is 12 months from delivery, except SOLAS safety equipment which
carries the manufacturer's certification period. No volume discount applies.
Returns are accepted within 30 days in original packaging. On-time delivery
performance for 2025 was 97 per cent.

6. CURRENCY AND PRICE VALIDITY
All framework prices are quoted in USD and are held for the calendar year.
Price increases require 60 days written notice and may not exceed 5 per cent in
any 12 month period.

7. DISPUTES AND LIABILITY
Claims for short delivery or damage must be raised within 7 days of receipt.
Supplier liability is limited to the invoice value of the affected goods.
Consequential losses, including vessel off-hire, are excluded in all cases.
