MERIDIAN MARITIME SERVICES - SHIP SPARES PROCUREMENT POLICY
Document PR-02, revision 5, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy governs the purchase of spare parts, consumables and safety
equipment for vessels in the Meridian managed fleet. It applies to the
superintendent, the technical purchasing team, and vessel masters raising
requisitions.

2. REQUISITION
Every purchase begins with a requisition raised in the fleet management system
against a vessel and a budget code. The requisition must state the part number,
the quantity, the required delivery port and the required delivery date.
Requisitions without a part number are returned to the originator.

3. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
Orders are approved according to total order value, excluding freight and duty:
- up to USD 5,000: Technical Superintendent
- USD 5,001 to USD 25,000: Fleet Operations Manager
- USD 25,001 to USD 100,000: Technical Director
- above USD 100,000: board approval is required
Splitting an order to stay below a threshold is a disciplinary matter.

4. COMPETITIVE QUOTATION
Three written quotations are required for any order above USD 10,000. Two
quotations are required between USD 2,500 and USD 10,000. Below USD 2,500 a
single quotation from an approved supplier is sufficient. Quotations must be
valid for at least 30 days.

5. APPROVED SUPPLIERS
Orders may only be placed with suppliers on the approved supplier list.
Suppliers are reviewed annually against delivery performance, quality
rejection rate, and commercial terms. A supplier whose on-time delivery falls
below 85 per cent is placed under review for 2 quarters.

6. EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT
Where a defect affects the safety of the vessel, its seaworthiness or its
compliance with class requirements, the Technical Superintendent may place an
order up to USD 25,000 without competitive quotation. The order must be
reported to the Fleet Operations Manager within 24 hours and ratified within 5
working days. Emergency procurement may not be used for routine stock
replenishment.

7. STOCK AND REORDER
Critical spares are held to the minimum levels defined in the vessel's
critical spares list. A reorder is triggered when stock falls to or below the
minimum order quantity. Items with a lead time above 30 days are reviewed
quarterly rather than on demand.

8. FREIGHT AND DELIVERY
Freight is quoted separately and is not included in the approval threshold
calculation. Delivery to a vessel in port requires 72 hours notice to the local
agent. Dangerous goods require an additional 5 working days for documentation.
